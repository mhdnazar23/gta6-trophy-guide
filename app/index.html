    Roadmap to the platinum

    Early guides estimate the story at about 60 hours and the platinum at about 200 hours. Both figures are estimates, not yet confirmed. The official list does not publish what 100% completion contains, so the last stage is a cleanup sweep, not a fixed list.

      Save habits that protect the platinum

      • Make a manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no confirmed mission replay for every moment yet.
      • Early guides report that cheats disable trophies, as in GTA V. Treat that as reported, not yet confirmed — do not use cheats on your platinum save.
      • Do side content as you pass it. Stranger strands, activities and collectibles are easier in the area than on a return trip.

      Missables

      There is no confirmed missable list yet. The game has only just released, and no early guide has published a verified list. This page will only list a trophy as missable when a source confirms it — until then, it lists the one reported case and the habit that protects you.

      Potentially missable — reported, not yet confirmed

      Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. Early guides (PlanetGTA6, OutputLag, KeenGamer) report that the balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 may not be revisit-able, because there is no mission replay for it. That is a report, not a confirmed fact.

      What to do: meditate at the balcony spot while you are still in Chapter 1, and make a manual save at the start of Chapter 1 before you do anything else there.

      Everything else

      Every other trophy in the official list is either a story trophy (unmissable — it pops when you finish the mission), an open-world activity, a collectible, a robbery or a money trophy. All of those can be done after the story, in free roam, as far as the official descriptions show. If a later guide confirms another missable, add it here — do not treat a rumour as a fact.

      Sources: Exophase for the official list; PlanetGTA6 / OutputLag / KeenGamer for the Chapter 1 report.
